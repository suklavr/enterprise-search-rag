# How do I assign a reinsurance treaty to policies?
Fill the Treaty Tag column in the portfolio with the treaty name; separate several treaties with semicolons. Define the treaties (Quota Share or Risk Excess of Loss) in the analysis settings under Reinsurance. Loss Metrics then report Gross, Net and Ceded.
