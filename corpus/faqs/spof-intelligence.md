# What is SPoF Intelligence and how is it different from Portfolio Manager?
SPoF Intelligence shows which single points of failure, such as cloud providers, software and service providers, your insureds depend on and how concentrated that dependency is. Portfolio Manager turns those dependencies into simulated losses through the catastrophe model.
