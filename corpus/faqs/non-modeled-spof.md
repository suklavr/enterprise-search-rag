# A SPoF I care about is not in the modeled list. What happens to it?
Each scenario models the SPoFs that together cover more than 90 percent of the relevant market. A SPoF outside the list is a Non-Modeled SPoF: it is not selected as an event target, although companies that depend on it can still be affected through the aggregate footprint of modeled SPoFs.
