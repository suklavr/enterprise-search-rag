# How do I see which companies in my portfolio were not matched?
Use the company information download, available in PM v6.30 and later: Export, Company Information in the application, or the API endpoint /odap/portfolios/export/v1/company-information/unmatched/ followed by the portfolio ID. Improve matching by supplying the company website or domain.
